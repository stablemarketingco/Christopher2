Our Estate Planning Services

Complete Estate Planning for South Jordan & Salt Lake County Families

Revocable Living Trusts

A properly drafted revocable living trust allows your estate to transfer to your heirs without going through Utah's probate process saving time, money, and family conflict. Ashvale Legal drafts trusts tailored specifically to your assets, your family structure, and Utah law.

Last Will & Testament

A will is the foundation of any estate plan. We draft legally binding wills that reflect your wishes for asset distribution, guardian designations for your minor children, and executor appointments specific to your situation, not a generic template.

Durable Power of Attorney

A durable power of attorney designates someone you trust to manage your financial and legal affairs if you're unable to do so. Critically important for South Jordan families and often overlooked until it's too late.

Advance Healthcare Directive

Also known as a living will, this document outlines your medical wishes and designates a healthcare agent to make decisions on your behalf. Ashvale Legal ensures your directive meets Utah's specific legal requirements.

Guardianship Designations

If you have minor children in Utah, a guardianship designation is essential. This document designates who will care for your children if something happens to you ensuring a Utah court follows your wishes, not state defaults.

Transfer-on-Death Deeds

A transfer-on-death deed allows your Utah real property to pass directly to a named beneficiary without probate. Ashvale Legal prepares and files TOD deeds for Salt Lake County properties with care and precision.

Pour-Over Wills & Trust Coordination

For clients with revocable living trusts, a pour-over will ensures that any assets not yet transferred into the trust at the time of death are directed there upon passing. We coordinate all documents to work together seamlessly.
