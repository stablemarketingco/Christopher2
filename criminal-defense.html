The Insider Advantage

We've Sat at the Prosecutor's Table. Now We're Sitting at Yours.

Before we were defense attorneys, we were on the other side of the courtroom — managing thousands of criminal cases as prosecutors in Salt Lake City. We made charging decisions. We built cases. We negotiated resolutions. We watched how evidence was assembled and where the weaknesses were, and we learned what it actually takes to move a case toward a just outcome.

When we crossed the aisle to defense work, we brought all of that with us. That experience is not a credential we list. It is the lens through which we evaluate every case we take — understanding what the prosecution is likely to prioritize, where the leverage exists, and how to build a defense strategy that addresses the actual vulnerabilities in the case against you.

A charge is not a conviction. What comes next depends on who is in your corner and what they understand about the system arrayed against you.

DUI & Impairment Defense

We Don't Just Argue Your DUI. We Challenge the Science Behind It.

DUI defense is not purely a legal question — it is a scientific one. As an ARIDE-certified firm, we evaluate the evidence in your case at the same level of technical expertise as the officer who conducted your roadside assessment.

That means we examine not just whether your rights were respected, but whether the field sobriety evaluation was administered correctly, whether the indicators relied upon actually support the conclusions drawn, and whether the charge against you holds up to genuine scrutiny.

Most DUI attorneys argue around the evidence. We argue through it.
